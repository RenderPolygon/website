about me

Hi! I'm Sasha, better known online as sasha_skye or RenderPolygon. I decided to make this website to show off my projects and what I'm capable of.

my interests

I like 3D modeling a lot. I'm good at Blender, and I enjoy making characters. I mostly model characters from video games or my own original characters.

I also really like UI design. That's why I decided to learn HTML and CSS in the first place. Specifically, with this website I tried recreating the "vibe" that websites of 2010's had. It's my favorite aesthetic.

I enjoy making games (whenever I have the motivation!). I know the Unity Editor pretty well, and I've made some games with it before. You can see one of my games, Don't Cross Me!, available on the projects page. I'm good at design, but not so much at coding/programming. That's why that game was "vibecoded", and I'm not proud of it. However, I'm willing to learn and become better!
